    Open a component in the gallery on the right (arrows and Enter, or a click) and its file appears on the left: a complete QmlTui app, imports and Terminal root included. Edit it and the gallery re-runs with that component open. Escape returns to the grid. Runtime: Qt for WebAssembly, GPLv3 (Qt is GPLv3 or commercial only in the browser).
