# Qamui Contributor Agreement

Qamui is offered under LGPL-3.0-only or GPL-3.0-only, with separate commercial
terms available upon inquiry. To preserve this model, the copyright holder must
hold rights to relicense every contribution. This agreement makes that
possible. It is modelled on the assignment form used by other multi-licensed
projects and is intentionally short.

**This text has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It must be before the
repository accepts its first external contribution.**

3. **Open-source promise.** The Project Owner will keep releasing Qamui,

4. **Your representations.** You represent that you are the author of the
   contribution, that you have the right to make this agreement (including,

5. **No warranty, no obligation.** The contribution is provided as is. The
   Project Owner is not obliged to include it.
6. **Scope.** This agreement covers every contribution you submit to Qamui
   from the date you first accept it, until you withdraw in writing.
   Withdrawal does not affect contributions already submitted.

## How to accept

Acceptance is recorded by the CLA check on the pull request. Comment
`I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA` on your first pull
request, or sign through the link the check provides.
